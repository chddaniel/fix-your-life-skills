# Complete Reset Protocol

Use these prompts selectively and conversationally. Preserve the order of the three phases, but follow strong user answers rather than mechanically asking every question.

## Checkpoint 1: morning excavation

Establish:

1. The low-grade dissatisfaction the user has accepted as normal
2. Their three most repeated complaints during the last year
3. What an observer might infer from their behavior rather than their claims
4. A truth about the current situation they avoid saying plainly
5. An ordinary weekday after five years on the same trajectory
6. The greater cost after ten years or at the end of life
7. A real person or visible trajectory that makes this future concrete
8. The identity, belonging, status, or safety that changing may threaten
9. The protection supplied by the current pattern and its price
10. An ordinary weekday in a desirable three-year future
11. A provisional identity consistent with that life
12. One proof action for the coming week

Close with a one-sentence anti-vision and a one-sentence minimum viable vision. If either sentence sounds impressive but not emotionally true, revise it in the user's own language.

## Checkpoint 2: day observations

Create four to six interrupts around real contexts. Capture one or two sentences for each:

- What am I avoiding through the current action?
- What goal would an observer infer from the previous two hours?
- Did that block move toward the anti-vision or vision?
- What important fact am I minimizing?
- What choice protected an old identity?
- When did I feel most engaged or depleted?

The times should be non-conflicting and safe. Missed prompts are data, not failure.

## Checkpoint 3: evening synthesis

Help the user name:

1. The best current explanation for being stuck
2. The internal pattern that deserves opposition
3. The compressed anti-vision
4. The provisional vision
5. One observable one-year outcome
6. One finishable one-month project
7. Two or three time-blockable actions for tomorrow
8. Constraints and sacrifices that are off-limits

## Final checkpoint

Return:

```markdown
# Life Reset

```

Do not promise that one day completes the change. The day produces a direction and the first feedback loop; later behavior tests it.
